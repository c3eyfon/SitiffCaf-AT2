Welcome to SitiffCafé

SitiffCafé is a cosy artisan café inspired by Japanese minimalism and Melbourne’s vibrant coffee culture. We serve small-batch roasted coffee, handcrafted pastries, and seasonal desserts—crafted fresh every day.

Whether you’re here for a quiet study session, a relaxed brunch, or to try our signature Sea Salt Caramel Mocha, we’ve created a warm space for you to unwind.
